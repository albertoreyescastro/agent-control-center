    01 / ROUTING

    Eligibility comes first.

    Capabilities, surface, health and policy constrain the route before an agent is selected.

    Why it matters

    An impressive model is still the wrong worker when its capability, availability or spend eligibility is wrong.

    02 / OWNERSHIP

    Failure has a boundary.

    Heartbeats reveal stale workers. Leases and attempt fences protect newer work from late results.

    Why it matters

    Durable state supports recovery. Circuit breakers and bounded retries prevent uncontrolled loops.

    03 / VERIFICATION

    Agents do not self-approve.

    Deterministic checks, an independent review role and a human merge gate separate output from authority.

    Why it matters

    Cross-family review is a design goal, not a measured claim in this simulation. Consequential changes require human approval.

    04 / SPEND SAFETY

    Recovery is not overage.

    Quota fences and distinct pools constrain fallback. Unknown quota stays unknown; paid usage is never implied.

    Why it matters

    No automatic paid refill or reset. Included-only eligibility requires provider-side verification outside this dashboard.
